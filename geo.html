01 / FIELD NOTES

Location is a hypothesis to test

Move from broad constraints to a small candidate set.

Begin with an observation sheet: visible roads, buildings, terrain, text and camera direction. Separate what is visibly present from interpretations such as “probably a school”. Use claimed region or language as a search bound, not a fact that must be true.

Generate several candidates using combinations of rare, stable features. Verify each with spatial relationships, then seek a different view or source. Start with exact geometry before polishing an address. If only a region is supported, report the region and the unresolved detail.

02 / FIELD NOTES

Region, scene and camera point are different results

Report at the resolution supported by the evidence.

LevelWhat is establishedWhat remains
RegionSeveral broad clues narrow the search areaSpecific site and viewpoint
Scene / landmarkRoads, water and structures match one siteExact side, elevation and camera position
ViewpointBearings, occlusion and perspective fit a position or short segmentExact coordinate precision and capture time

Image location, camera location and depicted subject can differ. A telephoto image may show a landmark far from the camera; a street panorama may have a displaced marker. Give a radius, segment or visible uncertainty when appropriate.

03 / FIELD NOTES

Try to break the preferred candidate

A hard spatial contradiction can outweigh many weak similarities.

Compare intersection order, which bank a building occupies, bridge alignment, rail curvature and the skyline behind the camera-facing side. A roof color may be common; a required river on the wrong side is a stronger contradiction. Before rejecting, check image mirroring, camera orientation and whether the map is from a different year.

Maintain “matches / contradicts / unverified” for every candidate. Lack of Street View is unverified, not a contradiction. Stop when the remaining result is sufficiently supported for your original question; do not manufacture certainty just because the search was long.
