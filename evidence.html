01 / FIELD NOTES

Separate the original, observation and inference

A future reader should be able to retrace each claim.

RecordKeep
Source IDStable internal ID, URL, publisher and accessible version
AcquisitionUTC retrieval time, method, response context and collector notes
FileOriginal filename, byte hash, format and a separate derived-copy path
ClaimExact observation, inference, alternatives and supporting / contradicting IDs
PresentationNecessary crop/redaction, transformation log and source attribution

An archive, screenshot and downloaded file serve different roles. A screenshot records visible rendering; it may omit full text or metadata. An archived page can fail to load a video. Verify that the saved artifact actually preserves the fact you plan to cite.

02 / FIELD NOTES

Hashing detects changed bytes, not a false caption

Integrity and authenticity are different properties.

Compute a SHA-256 digest when acquiring an original. Recompute it before later analysis to check whether the saved bytes changed. Keep transformed copies under new names and link them to their input. Identical hashes mean identical bytes; they do not prove the scene, creator, date or caption.

python3 -c "import hashlib,pathlib; print(hashlib.sha256(pathlib.Path('original.jpg').read_bytes()).hexdigest())"

Record handling steps. If the original platform has already recompressed a file, describe it as the platform-served copy rather than the camera original. A chain-of-custody log makes handling auditable but does not automatically settle legal admissibility.

Read the source: Berkeley Protocol · official PDF ↗

03 / FIELD NOTES

Preserve the context, then test replay

A saved URL is a pointer, not a preserved source.

Use Wayback to look for available historical captures and Webrecorder-style browser archiving where appropriate. Save visible attribution, capture time and the original URL. Reopen the result: dynamic maps, embedded media and authenticated resources may not replay completely.

Keep a private working archive and a deliberately selected public report. Remove session credentials and irrelevant personal details from public artifacts. Preserve the necessary geographic context around a match instead of cropping every screenshot to an isolated roof.

Read the source: Internet Archive Wayback Machine ↗ · Webrecorder ↗
