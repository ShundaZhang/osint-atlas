01 / FIELD NOTES

Project 1 · Verify a public landmark

Use your own photo or a clearly licensed public-landmark image.

Write an observation sheet before searching. Bound the region, generate at least three candidates, compare road–water–building relationships and reject alternatives with recorded reasons. Use a ground view and overhead view when both are available. Treat a missing view as an explicit gap.

Deliver an annotated image, candidate matrix, source ledger and a location result with uncertainty. Do not begin with a known exact pin: choose a picture whose answer can later be independently checked. The synthetic bridge exercise in Labs is a quick preparation.

02 / FIELD NOTES

Project 2 · Explain a visible land-cover change

Choose a large feature suited to the available satellite resolution.

Select a public reservoir, large construction site or broad vegetation area. Find comparable clear scenes before and after a visible change. Record product IDs, dates, band choices, cloud cover and alignment steps. Test seasonal or water-level explanations before calling the change permanent.

Deliver a side-by-side comparison, the earliest/latest observation bounds and a short explanation of what the sensor can establish. Keep causation and construction timing unresolved unless other independent records support them.

Read the source: Copernicus Browser guide ↗ · Sentinel-2 data guide ↗ · NASA Worldview ↗

03 / FIELD NOTES

Project 3 · Reconstruct a public organization’s source trail

Practice entity resolution without targeting a private individual.

Choose a public institution and one published claim about its own history or facilities. Trace official pages, accessible archived copies and registry records if applicable. Identify which publications reuse a common source and which independently support the claim.

Deliver a dated timeline, a small sourced relationship graph and a report separating observed records from interpretations. Use the downloadable case notebook for all three projects. The output is a reviewable investigation, not a leaderboard score or unsupported personal dossier.
