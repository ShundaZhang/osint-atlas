01 / FIELD NOTES

A coordinate needs order, units and a reference

Two numbers without labels are an incomplete location record.

Decimal latitude ranges from −90 to 90; longitude from −180 to 180. North/east are conventionally positive in signed decimal degrees. DMS uses degrees, minutes and seconds; apply the hemisphere sign to the complete value. More decimal places show numeric precision, not verified accuracy.

Interfaces differ: many map searches accept latitude, longitude; GeoJSON positions use longitude, latitude. A swap can be invalid, or both numbers can remain valid while pointing somewhere entirely different. Read the format contract and label fields explicitly.

{"type":"Point","coordinates":[120.0,30.0]}

02 / FIELD NOTES

Assigning a CRS and transforming coordinates are different

First determine what the source numbers mean.

WGS 84 geographic coordinates, projected Web Mercator metres and local projected coordinates use different models and units. QGIS can reproject correctly identified layers. Assigning EPSG:4326 to a metre-based file only changes the interpretation label; it does not magically convert the numbers.

GCJ-02 used by mainland-China mapping services and Baidu’s BD-09 need provider-aware handling. Do not overlay their raw numbers on a WGS 84 image and assume alignment. Consult provider documentation, keep the original system label and verify transformed results against multiple stable intersections.

03 / FIELD NOTES

Measure with an uncertainty appropriate to the data

A map pin, camera marker and survey control point have different reliability.

Use a geodesic distance for geographic coordinates rather than treating degree differences as metres. Projected measurements also depend on projection distortion. For small comparisons, a clearly labeled rough distance may suffice; for exact surveying, use a suitable reference, method and calibrated data.

A POI search can return a building centroid, entrance or district point. A georeferenced scan may fit selected ground-control points while drifting elsewhere. Check independent points and retain residuals and method notes. Report a viewpoint segment or area when the evidence does not justify one pin.
