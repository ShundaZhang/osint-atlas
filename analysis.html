Analysis
From clues to evidence

Alternative hypotheses, source independence and reporting

01 / FIELD NOTES

Compare hypotheses claim by claim

Use a matrix to see contradictions and missing checks.

List candidate hypotheses in columns and observations in rows. Mark whether each observation matches, contradicts or remains unverified. Give strong geometric contradictions explicit attention. Do not invent a numeric probability by adding arbitrary points to every clue.

Ask what would look different if the preferred explanation were wrong. Seek that disconfirming evidence and retain plausible alternatives. Several clues may come from the same image or underlying map dataset, so they are not automatically independent confirmations.

02 / FIELD NOTES

Describe confidence with its reasons and limits

Confidence is a judgment, not a substitute for evidence.

For this course, use “strongly supported” when multiple distinctive spatial relations fit, there is independent corroboration and important contradictions have been checked. Use “plausible” when meaningful features fit but decisive checks are missing. Use “unresolved” when alternatives remain. These are reporting conventions, not calibrated probabilities.

A confident place match may coexist with unknown time or creator. State confidence separately for each claim. Include what would change the conclusion, such as a dated uncropped frame or a ground view from the correct side.

03 / FIELD NOTES

Make the conclusion reviewable

A reader should see the claim, method, evidence and remaining uncertainty.

Lead with the supported answer and its scope. Follow with annotated comparisons retaining enough surrounding context, numbered sources, rejected alternatives and the method needed to reproduce the checks. Distinguish source statements from your own inferences.

A useful deliverable can say “The public landmark is identified; the camera lies somewhere along the marked road segment; the capture date remains unknown.” This is more informative than a precise coordinate and date with no supporting chain.
